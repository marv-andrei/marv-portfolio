About

I build pages that understand the ad.

I'm Marv — a Shopify landing page and funnel builder helping e-commerce brands create campaign-ready pages for paid ads, product launches, and CRO tests.

My background combines Shopify execution, Funnelish builds, Meta ads support, product page work, CRO research, and e-commerce operations. I'm especially useful for lean teams that need someone who can understand the marketing goal and also implement the page properly.

Most developers can build a page. But e-commerce landing pages need more than clean sections — they need to match the ad angle, explain the product clearly, build trust quickly, and make the next step obvious on mobile. That's the gap I close: I understand both the build side and the marketing side.

I came into e-commerce from a background as an aircraft maintenance technician, where precision, checklists, and systematic problem-solving were non-negotiable. That same discipline is what I bring to every build and QA pass. Read about that here →
