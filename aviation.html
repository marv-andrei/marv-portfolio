✈️ Aviation Background

Before e‑commerce, I worked on aircraft. The precision, discipline, and attention to detail from aviation is the foundation of everything I do today.

The hangar days
Aircraft Mechanic

I trained and worked as an aircraft mechanic — maintaining, inspecting, and repairing aircraft systems. In aviation, there's no room for "good enough." Every component, every fastener, every inspection has to be right.

That mindset — precision, checklists, systematic problem-solving — is what I bring to digital work. Whether it's debugging a Shopify theme or optimizing a checkout flow, the approach is the same: diagnose, fix, verify.

Aviation taught me that the best work is invisible. When everything runs smoothly, nobody notices. That's the goal.

  • Strict adherence to procedures & checklists
